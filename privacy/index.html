GDPR & Zero Data Retention

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026 · full-calendar-remastered.in

Full Calendar Remastered is a privacy-first calendar plugin for Obsidian. Core calendar synchronization and note management run locally on your device. We do not sell personal data, serve advertisements, or store your calendar events on our servers.

1. Local-First Core Plugin

  • Local Storage Only: Calendar configurations, OAuth tokens, and local notes stay within your Obsidian vault and device storage.
  • Direct Provider Sync: Google Calendar, Google Tasks, CalDAV, and ICS connections communicate directly over HTTPS/TLS between Obsidian and your chosen provider.
  • Transient OAuth Exchange: When using the default OAuth client, a stateless intermediary worker processes the authorization code solely in memory to complete login and immediately discards it.

2. Managed AI Service (Strictly GDPR Compliant & Zero Data Retention)

When you use the optional Calendar Agent in Free trial and managed plans mode (https://gateway.full-calendar-remastered.in), the service adheres to strict European GDPR data minimization and Zero Data Retention (ZDR) principles:

  • Zero Prompt & Calendar Content Storage: Prompts, assistant responses, and calendar event details are processed transiently in memory and forwarded to stateless EU-hosted AI inference endpoints with Zero Data Retention active and API model training disabled. No prompt text, completion text, or calendar event content is logged or persisted.
  • Local-Only Conversation History: Chat transcripts and staged event proposals are stored exclusively in your vault (.obsidian/plugins/full-calendar-remastered/agent/).
  • Cryptographic Device Binding: Devices authenticate using a locally generated Web Crypto ECDSA P-256 keypair stored in Obsidian’s SecretStorage. Paid license keys are stored server-side solely as one-way HMAC-SHA256 hashes.
  • Privacy-Safe Usage Accounting: To track Weighted Token Credit (WTC) balances and prevent automated trial abuse, our gateway records only aggregate token counts, credit ledger balances, and coarse daily subnet counters (IPv4 /24 or IPv6 /48). Raw client IP addresses are not stored.

3. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Mode

If you switch the Calendar Agent to BYOK mode, requests travel directly from your device to your chosen third-party or local endpoint (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Ollama, or LM Studio) without passing through our gateway, and that provider’s privacy policy applies.

4. Payments & Merchant of Record (Paddle)

Paid subscriptions (Pro and Power) are processed by Paddle as our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes payment details, billing address, email, and applicable VAT/sales tax in accordance with Paddle’s Privacy Policy. Full Calendar Remastered never receives or stores your credit card or banking information. Your email address is used solely to deliver your license key and essential subscription notices.

5. Cookieless Website & Operational Analytics

To understand overall site traffic, documentation usage, and service capacity purely for operational and logistics purposes, we use self-hosted, cookieless, privacy-first analytics (Umami). It does not set tracking cookies, does not follow you across websites, does not collect personal profiles, and never stores raw IP addresses.

6. Your Rights & Opt-Out

Under GDPR, you may request information about or deletion of any subscription license record associated with your email, deactivate your devices anytime inside Obsidian settings, or stop using Managed AI at any time.
