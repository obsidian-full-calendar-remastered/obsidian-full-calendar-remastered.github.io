Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about licensing, credits, privacy, and billing.

How do I activate my Pro or Power subscription in Obsidian?
Immediately after completing checkout, you receive an email containing your license key (fcr_live_...). Open Obsidian → Settings → Full Calendar Remastered → Integrations → Agent, paste the key into License key activation, and click Activate key.
Are my prompts or calendar events stored on your servers?
Never. Managed AI operates under strict GDPR data minimization and Zero Data Retention (ZDR) on stateless EU endpoints with API model training disabled. Only aggregate token counts and credit balances are stored for billing.
How are Weighted Token Credits (WTC) calculated?
1 uncached input token = 1 credit, 1 cached input token = 0.1 credits, and 1 output token = 4 credits. Your remaining balance is always visible inside Obsidian settings.
Can I use my license on multiple devices?
Yes. Pro supports up to 3 active devices and Power supports up to 5 active devices (desktop and mobile). You can deactivate an old device anytime in plugin settings to free up a slot.
Who handles payments, taxes, and cancellations?
All payments, localized currencies, VAT/sales tax, invoices, and subscription cancellations are handled securely by Paddle as our Merchant of Record. You can cancel anytime from your Paddle receipt or customer portal. See our Refund Policy.
What if I lose my license key, didn’t receive the email, or need a device reset?
Email from your checkout email address with your Subscription ID (sub_...) or Paddle receipt number. Because license keys are stored only as one-way hashes, we will securely rotate and re-issue a fresh key (or clear stuck device slots) while keeping your remaining credits intact.
